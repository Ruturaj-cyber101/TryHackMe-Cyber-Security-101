# Logs Fundamentals

## Room Overview
This room explains what logs are, why they are important, and how they are used to detect, investigate, and respond to security incidents.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What logs are and why they matter
- Common types of logs (system, application, security)
- Log collection and analysis basics
- Role of logs in threat detection

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Understanding log entries and events
- Identifying suspicious activity in logs
- Awareness of log sources used in SOCs
- Foundation for SIEM and monitoring tools

---

## SOC Relevance

Logs are essential for SOC analysts as they:
- Provide visibility into system activity
- Enable detection of attacks and anomalies
- Support incident investigations
- Form the backbone of SIEM-based monitoring
