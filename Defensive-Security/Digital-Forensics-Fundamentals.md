# Digital Forensics Fundamentals

## Room Overview
This room introduces the fundamentals of digital forensics and how evidence is collected, preserved, and analyzed during cyber investigations.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What digital forensics is
- Types of digital evidence
- Forensic investigation process
- Importance of evidence integrity

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Understanding how incidents are investigated
- Awareness of forensic artifacts
- Knowledge of evidence handling procedures
- Foundation for incident response investigations

---

## SOC Relevance

Digital forensics is important for SOC analysts as it:
- Supports incident investigations
- Helps analyze compromised systems
- Assists in identifying attacker activity
- Strengthens post-incident analysis skills
