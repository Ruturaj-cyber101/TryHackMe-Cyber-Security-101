# SOC Fundamentals

## Room Overview
This room introduces the fundamentals of a Security Operations Center (SOC) and explains how security teams monitor, detect, and respond to cyber threats.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What a SOC is and why it is important
- Roles and responsibilities of SOC analysts
- Security monitoring and alerting
- Incident response basics

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Understanding SOC workflows
- Awareness of common security alerts
- Knowledge of escalation processes
- Introduction to incident handling

---

## SOC Relevance

This room is essential for SOC analysts as it:
- Explains day-to-day SOC operations
- Builds understanding of detection and response
- Provides a foundation for SIEM and log analysis
