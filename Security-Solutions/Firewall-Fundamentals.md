# Firewall Fundamentals

## Room Overview
This room explains the fundamentals of firewalls and how they are used to control network traffic and protect systems from unauthorized access.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What a firewall is
- Types of firewalls (network, host-based)
- Allow and deny rules
- Inbound and outbound traffic filtering

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Understanding firewall rule logic
- Awareness of traffic filtering concepts
- Knowledge of firewall placement in networks
- Foundation for network security controls

---

## SOC Relevance

Firewalls are important for SOC analysts as they:
- Enforce network security policies
- Help block malicious traffic
- Provide logs for monitoring and investigation
- Support layered defense strategies
