# Introduction to SIEM

## Room Overview
This room introduces Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) systems and explains how they are used to collect, correlate, and analyze security events.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What SIEM is and why it is important
- Centralized log collection
- Event correlation and alerting
- Role of SIEM in SOC environments

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Understanding how logs are ingested into SIEM
- Awareness of alerts and dashboards
- Knowledge of SIEM use cases
- Foundation for security monitoring

---

## SOC Relevance

SIEM platforms are core to SOC operations as they:
- Provide visibility across systems and networks
- Enable detection of suspicious activity
- Support incident investigation and response
- Improve security monitoring efficiency
