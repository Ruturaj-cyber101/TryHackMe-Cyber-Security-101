# Cryptography Basics

## Room Overview
This room introduces the fundamentals of cryptography and its role in protecting data confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What cryptography is and why it is important
- Difference between encryption and encoding
- Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA triad)
- Real-world use of cryptography in security

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Understood how cryptography protects data
- Identified where encryption is used in systems and networks
- Built foundational knowledge for secure communication

---

## SOC Relevance

Cryptography knowledge helps SOC analysts to:
- Understand how data is protected
- Identify weak or misused encryption
- Support investigations involving secure communications
