# Hydra

## Room Overview
This room introduces Hydra, a fast and flexible password-cracking tool used to perform brute-force attacks against network services.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

- What Hydra is and when it is used
- Supported protocols and services
- Brute-force and password spraying concepts
- Importance of strong authentication controls

---

## Practical Skills Gained

- Executed Hydra against different services
- Used username and password wordlists
- Understood attack success and failure responses
- Learned limitations and risks of brute-force attacks

---

## SOC Relevance

Hydra knowledge helps SOC analysts to:
- Detect brute-force and password spraying attacks
- Analyze authentication logs
- Improve alerting and account protection strategies
